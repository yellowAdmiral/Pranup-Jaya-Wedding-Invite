Getting here

Coming from abroad? Fly to Bagdogra.

विदेशबाट आउने पाहुनाहरूका लागि

Bagdogra is the airport for Darjeeling. Land there and we will meet you at arrivals and drive you up into the hills.

बागडोगरा विमानस्थलसम्म आउनुहोस्, त्यहाँबाट हामी तपाईंलाई लिन आउनेछौं।

  1. Fly into India

    Take an international flight to Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai or Bengaluru. Most visitors need an Indian visa, and many nationalities can apply online at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Citizens of Nepal and Bhutan do not need one. Darjeeling needs no extra permit.

    दिल्ली, कोलकाता, मुम्बई वा बेङ्गलुरुसम्म अन्तर्राष्ट्रिय उडान लिनुहोस्।

  2. Connect to Bagdogra

    Direct domestic flights link all four cities to Bagdogra (airport code IXB). Drukair also flies there from Bangkok and Paro.

    त्यहाँबाट बागडोगरा (IXB) को सिधा उडान लिनुहोस्।

  3. We pick you up

    Add your flight number and landing time to your RSVP. One of us will be waiting at arrivals to drive you to Darjeeling.

    आफ्नो उडानको विवरण पठाउनुहोस्, हामी विमानस्थलमै तपाईंलाई भेट्न आउनेछौं।

Arrive by 9 February. The wedding starts at 11 AM on the 10th, and the drive up takes most of a morning.

Land by early afternoon if you can, so the hill road is done in daylight.

Bring warm layers. February in Darjeeling is cold, especially at night.
